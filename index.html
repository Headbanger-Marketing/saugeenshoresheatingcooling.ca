FAQs

HVAC Questions from Saugeen Shores Homeowners

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from Port Elgin, Southampton, and Bruce County customers.

Can you service the propane furnace at my Port Elgin home?

Yes. A large share of homes and cottages around Saugeen Shores run propane rather than natural gas, and propane furnace repair is some of the most common work we do. Our technicians are licensed for propane systems and coordinate with your fuel supplier so you are never left waiting on a delivery in the middle of a cold snap.

Do you handle seasonal cottage start-ups and shut-downs in Southampton?

We do. For cottages that sit empty between visits, we set up seasonal start-up and shut-down visits so the place is warm when you arrive for the weekend and the plumbing is protected when you leave. We can also pair that with a smart thermostat so you can warm the cottage up from your phone before you drive out from the city.

I want to replace my old oil furnace. Can you do a heat-pump conversion?

Yes. We install cold-climate heat pumps that fully replace an oil furnace, handle the tank removal and the rebate paperwork, and size the system to carry your entire heating load through a Bruce County winter, not just the shoulder seasons. A lot of lakeshore homes have made exactly this switch with us.

How fast can you get to a no-heat call in Kincardine?

Because we are based right in Port Elgin, Kincardine and the surrounding lakeshore are part of our regular service area, and we prioritize no-heat calls. Response time depends on the day's call volume, but our goal is to get a technician to you the same day during a cold snap, with honest, upfront pricing before any work begins.

Will a ductless mini-split actually heat a cottage addition through the winter?

Properly sized, yes. Today's cold-climate mini-splits pull heat out of the air well below freezing, so one quiet unit can heat and cool a sunroom, addition, or lakeside workshop year-round. We match the BTUs to the actual space so it dehumidifies properly in summer and keeps up through a January cold snap.

How much does a service call cost?

Quotes are free and carry no obligation. For a repair visit, we diagnose the problem first and explain every cost clearly before any work begins, so you decide what gets done and what it will cost. No surprise add-ons and no padded invoices.
